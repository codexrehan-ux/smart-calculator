# OmniCalculator Pro (Universal Smart Calculator Suite)

A comprehensive, multi-mode collection of **34+ high-performance online calculators** for finance, health, business, education, everyday math, and scientific operations.

Everything runs **100% in your browser** with zero external server dependencies, zero tracking, and instant reactive computation.

---

## 🚀 Features at a Glance

- **⚡ 34+ Specialized Calculators:** Full coverage across 7 key categories.
- **🔍 Global ⌘K / Ctrl+K Command Palette:** Search any calculator instantly by name, keyword, or formula.
- **🌓 Dark & Light Mode:** Seamless theme switching with persistent settings.
- **⏱️ Live Calculation History:** Save and review recent calculations locally.
- **📋 One-Click Copy:** Copy calculated results and formulas straight to clipboard.
- **📱 100% Responsive & Accessible:** Optimized for desktop, tablet, and mobile browsers.

## 🧮 Calculator Directory

### 1. 🏠 Loans & EMI
- **Loan Calculator**: Monthly payments, total interest, and amortization cost.
- **Mortgage Calculator**: Home value, down payment, interest, taxes, and insurance breakdown.
- **EMI Calculator**: Equated monthly installments with principal vs. interest visual ratio.

### 2. 📈 Interest & Savings
- **Compound Interest Calculator**: Compounding frequency (monthly, quarterly, daily, annual) and growth forecasting.
- **Simple Interest Calculator**: Non-compounding loans and fixed bonds ($I = P \cdot r \cdot t$).
- **Retirement Planner**: Nest egg accumulation and 4% safe withdrawal rule.
- **SIP Calculator**: Systematic investment plan returns for mutual funds and equity.
- **FD Calculator**: Fixed deposit maturity value and effective annual yield.

### 3. 💼 Business & Profitability
- **ROI Calculator**: Return on investment %, net profit, and annualized CAGR.
- **Break-Even Calculator**: Fixed/variable cost analysis and target sales volume.
- **Margin Calculator**: Gross profit and profit margin percentage.
- **Markup Calculator**: Retail pricing and markup percentage over cost.
- **Inflation Calculator**: Purchasing power impact and future cost of goods.

### 4. 🧮 Everyday & Math
- **Interactive Scientific & Standard Calculator**: Trigonometry ($\sin, \cos, \tan$), logarithms ($\log, \ln$), powers ($x^y, \sqrt{}$), factorial ($x!$), memory ($M+, MR$).
- **Percentage Calculator**: $X\%$ of $Y$, $X$ is what $\%$ of $Y$, and percentage change.
- **Tip & Bill Splitter**: Custom tip percentage and per-person cost sharing.
- **Discount Calculator**: Percent off, sales tax calculation, and net savings.
- **Salary & Wage Converter**: Convert hourly rate, weekly pay, monthly salary, and annual pay.
- **Income Tax Calculator**: US Federal and India New Tax Regime (FY 2025-26) estimates.
- **Fuel Cost & Trip Calculator**: Fuel consumption, trip costs, and efficiency.
- **Multi-Unit Converter**: Length, Weight, Temperature, Speed, and Digital Storage.

### 5. 🏃 Health & Fitness
- **BMI Calculator**: Body Mass Index and healthy weight ranges (Metric & Imperial).
- **Calorie & TDEE Calculator**: BMR (Mifflin-St Jeor) and daily calorie targets.
- **Body Fat Calculator**: U.S. Navy circumference method.
- **Water Intake Calculator**: Optimal hydration volume based on body weight and activity.
- **Pregnancy Due Date Calculator**: Estimated due date, gestational age, and trimester tracker.
- **Running Pace Calculator**: Pace (min/km, min/mi) and race finish times (5K, 10K, Half, Full).
- **Ovulation & Fertility Calculator**: Fertile window prediction and cycle dates.
- **Sleep Cycle Calculator**: 90-minute REM sleep cycle optimization.

### 6. 🎓 Education
- **GPA Calculator**: Grade Point Average on 4.0, 5.0, or 10.0 scale with credit weighting.
- **CGPA to Percentage Converter**: Standard conversions (CBSE 9.5 factor, VTU, KTU, AICTE).

### 7. 📅 Date & Time
- **Exact Age Calculator**: Years, months, days, total hours, and birthday countdown.
- **Date Difference Calculator**: Calendar days, weeks, and months between dates.
- **Time Duration & Timesheet**: Hours and decimal time for freelance timesheets.
